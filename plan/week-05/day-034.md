# Day 34 · Week 5 · First measurement

**Time:** about 2.5 hours

## Why today matters
Inference engineering is driven by measurement. Today you record your first baseline, the number every later improvement is compared with.

## Tasks
- [ ] Generate 256 tokens in bfloat16 and measure tokens per second. Call `torch.cuda.synchronize()` before reading the timer, or your timing will be wrong.
- [ ] Measure the time of each step separately and plot step time against step number.
- [ ] Create `RESULTS.md` with the date, GPU, model, dtype and numbers.
- [ ] Explain in your notes why each step gets slower as the text grows. (Hint: day 19.)

## Resources
