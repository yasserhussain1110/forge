# Day 137 · Week 20 · GPTQ and AWQ

**Time:** about 3 hours (plus 30 minutes of coding practice)

## Why today matters
Naive 4-bit quantization hurts quality noticeably. GPTQ and AWQ are the two most widely used methods to fix that, and most 4-bit checkpoints you'll find use one of them.

## Tasks
- [ ] Read the GPTQ paper's introduction and method, at the level of intuition: quantize weights one column at a time and adjust the remaining ones to compensate for the error.
- [ ] Read the AWQ paper's introduction and method: protect the weights that matter most by looking at activation sizes.
- [ ] Download an AWQ 4-bit version of Llama 3.1 8B from Hugging Face and inspect its tensors: packed weights, scales, zero points, group size.

## Resources
- [GPTQ paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.17323)
- [AWQ paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.00978)

## Done when
You can explain how 8 4-bit values are packed into one 32-bit integer in the checkpoint.
