# Day 134 · Week 20 · Quantization, visually

**Time:** about 2.5 hours (plus 30 minutes of coding practice)

## Why today matters
Decode speed is limited by how many bytes of weights you read per token. Storing weights in 8 or 4 bits instead of 16 cuts those bytes by 2–4×, if you can keep quality.

## Tasks
- [ ] Read Maarten Grootendorst's "A Visual Guide to Quantization".
- [ ] Learn the vocabulary: scale, zero point, symmetric versus asymmetric, per-tensor versus per-channel versus per-group, weight-only versus weight-and-activation.
- [ ] Quantize one weight matrix of your model to 8 bits and to 4 bits in plain PyTorch, dequantize it, and measure the error for each granularity.

## Resources

## Done when
You have an error table for different bit widths and granularities.
