# Day 2 · Week 1 · Tensor shapes and broadcasting

**Time:** about 2.5 hours

## Why today matters
Most bugs in model code are shape bugs. Being able to predict shapes in your head is the single most useful skill for the next 6 months.

## Tasks
- [ ] Read the PyTorch broadcasting rules.
- [ ] Practice indexing and slicing: `x[0]`, `x[:, 1]`, `x[..., -1]`, `x[None]`.
- [ ] Practice `view`, `reshape`, `transpose`, `permute`, `unsqueeze`, `squeeze`, `cat`, `stack`.
- [ ] Practice matrix multiply with `@` on 2-D and 3-D tensors (batched matmul).
- [ ] Write 20 small exercises. For each, **predict the output shape on paper first**, then run it. Record how many you got right.

## Resources
- [PyTorch broadcasting semantics](https://pytorch.org/docs/stable/notes/broadcasting.html)
- [PyTorch tensor views](https://pytorch.org/docs/stable/tensor_view.html)

## Done when
You predict at least 18 of 20 shapes correctly.
