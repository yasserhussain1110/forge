# Day 5 · Week 1 · Your first trained model

**Time:** about 3 hours

## Why today matters
Even though you'll build inference (running models), you need to know how models are trained to understand what the weights are.

## Tasks
- [ ] Do the **Datasets & DataLoaders**, **Build the Neural Network**, **Automatic Differentiation** and **Optimizing Model Parameters** sections of Learn the Basics.
- [ ] Train the FashionMNIST classifier end to end and record the final accuracy.
- [ ] Print the model's parameters: names and shapes. Count the total number of parameters.
- [ ] Save the model with `torch.save(model.state_dict(), ...)` and load it back. Look at what a `state_dict` is (a dictionary from names to tensors). Real LLM checkpoints are the same idea.

## Resources
- [PyTorch: Learn the Basics](https://pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/basics/intro.html)

## Done when
You have a trained model, you know its parameter count, and you understand that "loading a model" means filling named tensors.
