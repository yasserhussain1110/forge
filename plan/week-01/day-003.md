# Day 3 · Week 1 · Linear algebra intuition, part 1

**Time:** about 2 hours

## Why today matters
A neural network is mostly matrix multiplications. You don't need to prove theorems, but you need a picture of what a matrix does to a vector.

## Tasks
- [ ] Watch chapters 1–4 of 3Blue1Brown's Essence of Linear Algebra: vectors, linear combinations, linear transformations, matrix multiplication.
- [ ] After each chapter, pause and write a 3-sentence summary in `notes/day-003.md`.
- [ ] Do a 2×2 matrix times a 2-vector by hand, then check it in PyTorch.
