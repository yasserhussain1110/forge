# Day 4 · Week 1 · Linear algebra intuition, part 2

**Time:** about 2 hours

## Why today matters
Attention, the heart of a transformer, scores how related two tokens are using a dot product. Today you build intuition for why that works.

## Tasks
- [ ] Watch chapters 7–9 of Essence of Linear Algebra (inverse matrices, nonsquare matrices, dot products).
- [ ] In PyTorch, create a few vectors and compute dot products and cosine similarity between them. Observe: similar directions give large values.
- [ ] Compute how many multiply-add operations a `(M×K) @ (K×N)` matmul needs. Write the formula in your notes (you'll use it constantly later).

## Resources
- [3Blue1Brown: Essence of Linear Algebra](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/linear-algebra)

## Done when
You can explain why the dot product measures similarity, and you know that a matmul costs about 2·M·K·N floating-point operations.
