# Day 6 · Week 1 · Probability, softmax and sampling

**Time:** about 2.5 hours

## Why today matters
An LLM outputs a score for every word in its vocabulary. Softmax turns those scores into probabilities, and sampling picks the next word. You'll implement this yourself in week 6.

## Tasks
- [ ] Read Dive into Deep Learning section 2.6 (probability basics).
- [ ] Read the softmax regression section (chapter 4, "Softmax Regression") and the part on cross-entropy loss.
- [ ] In PyTorch: take a vector of 10 scores, apply `torch.softmax`, check it sums to 1.
- [ ] Sample 10,000 times from that distribution with `torch.multinomial` and check the frequencies match the probabilities.
- [ ] Divide the scores by 0.5 and by 2.0 before softmax and see how the distribution gets sharper or flatter. This is "temperature".
