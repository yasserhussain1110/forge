# Day 129 · Week 19 · Greedy speculative decoding

**Time:** about 3 hours (plus 30 minutes of coding practice)

## Tasks
- [ ] Load two models that share a tokenizer (required) in the engine. On your 8 GB GPU, use Llama 3.2 1B as the draft model and Llama 3.2 1B Instruct as the target. Being the same size, they give no speed-up, but they disagree often enough to exercise rejection and rollback, which is what today is about. A realistic pair (1B drafting for Llama 3.1 8B) needs 24 GB or more; day 131 covers that without renting.
- [ ] Implement greedy speculative decoding for a single request: the draft proposes k tokens, the target scores all of them in one forward pass, keep the longest prefix that matches the target's own greedy choice, plus one token from the target.
- [ ] Roll back both KV caches correctly when guesses are rejected. This is the hard part.
- [ ] Test: output must exactly equal the target model's normal greedy output.

## Done when
Greedy speculative output matches normal greedy output exactly.
