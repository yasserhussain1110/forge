# Day 127 · Week 19 · Speculative decoding, the idea

**Time:** about 3 hours (plus 30 minutes of coding practice)

## Why today matters
At low load the GPU is underused during decode. Speculative decoding lets a small, fast model guess several tokens, then the big model checks all the guesses in one step. You get several tokens for the price of about one big-model step, with exactly the same output quality.
