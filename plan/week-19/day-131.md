# Day 131 · Week 19 · Measure acceptance and speed-up

**Time:** about 3 hours (plus 30 minutes of coding practice)

## Tasks
- [ ] With your local model pair, measure acceptance rate for k = 1 to 8 on two kinds of prompts: code and casual chat.
- [ ] A real speed-up needs a target much bigger than the draft, which doesn't fit in 8 GB. Instead, predict it: use the day-127 formula, your measured acceptance rates, and your day-45 bandwidth limit to estimate the speed-up of Llama 3.2 1B drafting for Llama 3.1 8B at 1, 8 and 32 concurrent requests. Explain why the benefit shrinks as load grows.

Otherwise skip it; tomorrow's prompt-lookup drafting gives you a real speed-up measurement on your own GPU.
- [ ] Record everything in `RESULTS.md`, labelling predictions clearly as predictions.
