# Day 117 · Week 17 · Chunked prefill, the idea

**Time:** about 2.5 hours (plus 30 minutes of coding practice)

## Why today matters
When a long prompt arrives, its prefill can take hundreds of milliseconds, and every other user's stream freezes meanwhile. Chunked prefill splits long prompts into pieces and mixes them with decode steps.

## Tasks
- [ ] Read the Sarathi-Serve paper, focusing on the problem statement, chunked prefills, and "stall-free" scheduling.
- [ ] Reproduce the problem first: run your benchmark with steady chat traffic, then inject a few 8,000-token prompts and record the TPOT spikes of the other users.
- [ ] Write in your notes how a per-step "token budget" balances TTFT against TPOT.
