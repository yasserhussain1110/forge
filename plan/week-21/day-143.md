# Day 143 · Week 21 · PyTorch distributed basics

**Time:** about 3 hours (plus 30 minutes of coding practice)

## Why today is local
You'll rent two GPUs only once, on day 145. Until then, develop on your own machine: `torchrun` can start two processes on one machine, and the `gloo` backend lets them talk even when they share one GPU (NCCL, the fast GPU backend, refuses to put two processes on the same GPU).

## Tasks
- [ ] Do the "Writing Distributed Applications with PyTorch" tutorial, using the `gloo` backend.
- [ ] Write a script launched with `torchrun --nproc_per_node=2` that runs an all-reduce on a large tensor and measures the achieved bandwidth. Make the backend a command-line option, and test it locally with `gloo`.
- [ ] Add it to a `scripts/rental-session/` checklist of everything to run on day 145. The real NCCL bandwidth number comes from that session.
