# Day 20 · Week 3 · Tokenization

**Time:** about 3 hours

## Why today matters
Models don't see text; they see token IDs. Your server will have to convert text to tokens and back, including tricky cases like emoji split across tokens.

## Tasks
- [ ] Watch "Let's build the GPT Tokenizer" (it's long; watching at 1.25× is fine).
- [ ] Understand byte-pair encoding (BPE): how merges are learned and applied.
- [ ] Use the `tiktoken` library or a Hugging Face tokenizer to encode a sentence that contains an emoji. Look at the token IDs, then decode them one at a time and see what happens to the emoji.
- [ ] Write in your notes why decoding token by token can produce broken characters.
