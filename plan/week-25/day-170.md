# Day 170 · Week 25 · Design an LLM serving platform

**Time:** about 3 hours (plus 30 minutes of coding practice)

## Tasks
- [ ] Read the chapters on deployment and model serving in *Designing Machine Learning Systems* by Chip Huyen.
- [ ] Practice, on a whiteboard or paper with a 45-minute timer: "Design a platform that serves several LLMs to many customers at 10,000 requests per second."
- [ ] Cover: capacity estimation (GPUs needed, using your own measured numbers), routing, autoscaling, prefix-cache-aware load balancing, multi-region, failure handling, cost, and monitoring.
- [ ] Afterwards, write up your design and list where you got stuck.
