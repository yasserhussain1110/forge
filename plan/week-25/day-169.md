# Day 169 · Week 25 · Your staff engineer story

**Time:** about 3 hours (plus 30 minutes of coding practice)

## Why today matters
Staff-level interviews test scope and judgement as much as technical skill: how you choose problems, influence others and make trade-offs. You need stories ready.

## Tasks
- [ ] Read the first part of *Staff Engineer* by Will Larson (the archetypes and "what do staff engineers do"). Much of it is also free on staffeng.com.
- [ ] Decide which archetype fits you best and why.
- [ ] Write 5 stories in the STAR format (situation, task, action, result), covering: a hard technical decision, a time you changed a team's direction, a failure, a time you mentored someone, and Forge itself.
- [ ] For each story, note the measurable result.
