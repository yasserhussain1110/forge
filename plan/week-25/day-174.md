# Day 174 · Week 25 · Your Forge deep-dive talk

**Time:** about 3 hours (plus 30 minutes of coding practice)

## Why today matters
Many staff-level interview loops include a "deep dive" or "past project" round where you present something you built and get grilled on it. Forge is that project.

## Tasks
- [ ] Make about 10 slides for a 10-minute talk: problem, architecture, three key technical decisions with their trade-offs, results, what you'd do next.
- [ ] Prepare for the hard follow-up questions. Write answers to at least 15, such as: "Why is your p99 worse than vLLM's?", "How would this scale to 100 GPUs?", "What would you change about your scheduler?", "How do you know your output is correct?"
- [ ] Rehearse it out loud twice and time it.

## Done when
You can deliver the talk in 10 minutes and answer your 15 questions confidently.
