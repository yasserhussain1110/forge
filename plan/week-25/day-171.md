# Day 171 · Week 25 · Design multi-adapter (LoRA) serving

**Time:** about 3 hours (plus 30 minutes of coding practice)

## Why today matters
Companies like Databricks let customers fine-tune models with small "LoRA adapters" and serve thousands of them on shared GPUs. It's a common interview topic.

## Tasks
- [ ] Learn what LoRA is from the Hugging Face PEFT documentation's conceptual guide.
- [ ] Read the S-LoRA paper's introduction and design sections.
- [ ] Practice with a 45-minute timer: "Design a service that serves 10,000 fine-tuned variants of one base model."
- [ ] Cover: batching requests that use different adapters, loading adapters between GPU and CPU memory, scheduling, and isolation between customers.
- [ ] Write up your design.
