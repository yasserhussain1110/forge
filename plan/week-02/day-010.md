# Day 10 · Week 2 · makemore part 1: the bigram model

**Time:** about 3 hours

## Why today matters
This is your first language model. It predicts the next character from the current one, which is the same idea an LLM uses, just much simpler.

## Tasks
- [ ] Watch "The spelled-out intro to language modeling: building makemore" and code along.
- [ ] Generate 20 names from your trained bigram model.
- [ ] Note the two key ideas: the model outputs a probability distribution over the next token, and generation samples from it repeatedly in a loop.
