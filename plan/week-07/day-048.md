# Day 48 · Week 7 · Serving metrics

**Time:** about 2 hours

## Why today matters
Users don't care about tokens per second on one request. They care about how fast the first word appears and how smoothly the rest streams. Production teams measure these.

## Tasks
- [ ] Read Databricks' "LLM Inference Performance Engineering: Best Practices".
- [ ] Define in your notes: time to first token (TTFT), time per output token (TPOT), end-to-end latency, throughput, and why the 99th percentile (p99) matters more than the average.
- [ ] Read the Lilian Weng post on inference optimization as a map of everything you'll build in the coming months. Don't try to understand all of it yet.
