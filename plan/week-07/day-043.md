# Day 43 · Week 7 · Compute, memory and overhead

**Time:** about 2.5 hours

## Why today matters
Every slowdown in deep learning comes from one of three places: doing math (compute), moving data (memory bandwidth), or Python and framework overhead. Knowing which one you're fighting decides what to fix.
