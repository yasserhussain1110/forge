# Day 44 · Week 7 · Rooflines

**Time:** about 3 hours

## Tasks
- [ ] Read the "Rooflines" chapter of How to Scale Your Model.
- [ ] Read its chapter on transformer inference.
- [ ] Learn "arithmetic intensity": FLOPs performed per byte moved from memory. Calculate it for a matrix-vector multiply (decode with batch size 1) and for a large matrix-matrix multiply (prefill).
- [ ] Look up your GPU's peak FLOPs and memory bandwidth on its datasheet, and compute its "ridge point" (peak FLOPs ÷ bandwidth).

## Resources
- [How to Scale Your Model](https://jax-ml.github.io/scaling-book/)
