# Day 46 · Week 7 · The PyTorch profiler

**Time:** about 3 hours

## Tasks
- [ ] Do the PyTorch profiler recipe.
- [ ] Profile 20 decode steps of your engine. Sort operations by total CUDA time and by CPU time.
- [ ] Export a Chrome trace and open it in `chrome://tracing` or [Perfetto](https://ui.perfetto.dev/).
- [ ] Find the top 5 most expensive operations and write them in your notes.
- [ ] Look for gaps in the GPU timeline where it waits on the CPU.

## Resources
- [PyTorch profiler recipe](https://pytorch.org/tutorials/recipes/recipes/profiler_recipe.html)

## Done when
You have a trace and a top-5 list.
