# Day 45 · Week 7 · Your theoretical speed limit

**Time:** about 2 hours

## Tasks
- [ ] Calculate the fastest possible decode speed at batch size 1: every step must read all the weights once, so the maximum tokens per second ≈ memory bandwidth ÷ model size in bytes.
- [ ] Do this for Llama 3.2 1B in bfloat16 on your GPU.
- [ ] Compare with your measured tokens per second. What percentage of the limit do you reach?
- [ ] Record both numbers in `RESULTS.md`. This "percentage of the roofline" is how professionals talk about efficiency.

## Done when
You know your efficiency percentage. Expect it to be low; that's the point of the next weeks.
