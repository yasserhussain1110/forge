# Day 54 · Week 8 · Compare with vLLM

**Time:** about 2.5 hours

## Why today matters
vLLM is the industry-standard open-source engine. Measuring your gap against it tells you what to build next and gives your project an honest yardstick.

## Tasks
- [ ] Install vLLM on the same GPU and serve the same model (`vllm serve meta-llama/Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct`).
- [ ] Run your exact benchmark against it at 1, 4 and 16 concurrent requests.
- [ ] Make a table comparing Forge and vLLM. Expect vLLM to be far ahead, especially with many requests.
- [ ] For each gap, write your best guess at the cause. You'll revisit these guesses throughout the project.
