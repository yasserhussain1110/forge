# Day 59 · Week 9 · CUDA graphs, the idea

**Time:** about 2.5 hours

## Why today matters
On day 47 you saw the GPU waiting on the CPU between small kernels. CUDA graphs record a whole sequence of kernels once and replay it with a single launch, removing that overhead.
