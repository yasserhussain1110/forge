# Day 99 · Week 15 · The PagedAttention paper, part 1

**Time:** about 3 hours (plus 30 minutes of coding practice)

## Why today matters
This paper introduced vLLM. Its idea, borrowed from how operating systems manage memory, is the foundation of every modern serving engine.

## Tasks
- [ ] Read sections 1–4 of "Efficient Memory Management for Large Language Model Serving with PagedAttention".
- [ ] Understand the three kinds of waste in a contiguous cache: reserved-but-unused space, internal fragmentation, external fragmentation.
- [ ] Understand the core idea: split the KV cache into fixed-size blocks, and give each sequence a "block table" mapping its logical blocks to physical blocks anywhere in memory.
