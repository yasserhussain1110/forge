# Day 83 · Week 12 · Why decode attention is different

**Time:** about 2.5 hours

## Why today matters
During decode there is only one query token, so a prefill-style kernel leaves most of the GPU idle. Flash-Decoding fixes this by also splitting the work along the cached keys and values.
