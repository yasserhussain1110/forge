# Day 179 · Week 26 · Reach out to inference teams

**Time:** about 3 hours

## Why today matters
Referrals and direct messages to engineers convert far better than applying cold. Your blog posts give you a genuine reason to reach out.

## Tasks
- [ ] Make a list of 15–20 engineers and managers on inference and serving teams: Databricks (Model Serving, Mosaic AI), Google (Gemini serving, TPU and XLA teams), Meta (PyTorch, Llama inference), plus others you like (Anyscale, Together AI, Fireworks, NVIDIA).
- [ ] Prioritize people you've interacted with during the project: maintainers who reviewed your pull requests, people who commented on your posts or in GPU MODE.
- [ ] Send short, specific messages: one sentence on who you are, one link to your most relevant post, and one specific question or ask (for example a 20-minute chat or a referral).
- [ ] Track everything in a spreadsheet.

## Done when
At least 15 messages are sent.
