# Day 121 · Week 18 · Metrics and observability

**Time:** about 3 hours (plus 30 minutes of coding practice)

## Why today matters
Real services are run by watching dashboards. Exposing the right metrics shows you think like someone who has been on call.

## Tasks
- [ ] Read the `prometheus_client` Python library's docs.
- [ ] Expose a `/metrics` endpoint with: requests waiting and running, KV cache usage percentage, prefix cache hit rate, preemption count, and histograms of TTFT, TPOT and end-to-end latency.
- [ ] Run Prometheus and Grafana locally (Docker is easiest) and build a small dashboard.
- [ ] Take a screenshot of the dashboard during a load test for your README.

## Resources
