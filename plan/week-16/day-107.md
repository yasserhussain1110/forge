# Day 107 · Week 16 · Preemption, the idea

**Time:** about 2.5 hours (plus 30 minutes of coding practice)

## Why today matters
Running sequences keep growing. Eventually the cache runs out of blocks mid-generation, and the engine must pause someone. How it does that decides whether the server degrades gracefully or falls over.

## Tasks
- [ ] Read section 4.5 of the PagedAttention paper ("Scheduling and Preemption").
- [ ] Read the vLLM documentation's page on preemption and its trade-offs.
- [ ] Compare the two strategies in your notes: swapping blocks to CPU memory versus throwing them away and recomputing later.
- [ ] Decide which request to preempt (vLLM uses the most recently arrived one; think about why) and add it to your scheduler design doc.
