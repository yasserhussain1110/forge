# Day 92 · Week 14 · Why continuous batching matters

**Time:** about 2.5 hours (plus 30 minutes of coding practice)

## Why today matters
Decode at batch size 1 is memory-bound: the GPU reads all the weights to produce one token. If you process many users' tokens in the same step, you read the weights once for all of them. This is where vLLM's big lead over you comes from.

## Tasks
