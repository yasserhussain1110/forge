# Day 87 · Week 13 · FlashAttention-3 and newer GPUs

**Time:** about 2 hours

## Why today matters
You won't implement FlashAttention-3, but interviewers at big companies run H100 and newer GPUs. You should know what those GPUs add and why it matters.

## Tasks
- [ ] Skim the FlashAttention-3 paper: the introduction, the figures, and the section on Hopper features.
- [ ] Write in your notes, one sentence each: warp specialization, asynchronous memory copies (TMA), FP8 tensor cores.
- [ ] Write a short list of "things I know exist but haven't built". Being honest about this list is a strength in interviews.
