# Day 74 · Week 11 · Your fused RMSNorm kernel

**Time:** about 3 hours

## Tasks
- [ ] Do Triton tutorial 05, "Layer Normalization", and read only the forward pass.
- [ ] Write your own Triton RMSNorm kernel for Forge. Bonus: fuse the residual add that comes right before it.
- [ ] Test it against your PyTorch RMSNorm for several shapes and dtypes.
- [ ] Benchmark it against the PyTorch version and against the torch.compile version.

## Resources
- [Triton tutorials](https://triton-lang.org/main/getting-started/tutorials/index.html)
- Look at how [Liger Kernel](https://github.com/linkedin/Liger-Kernel) implements RMSNorm in Triton, after you've written your own.

## Done when
Your kernel passes tests and you know whether it beats torch.compile.
