# Day 22 · Week 4 · Decoder-only models and generation

**Time:** about 2 hours

## Tasks
- [ ] Read The Illustrated GPT-2, focusing on the sections about generating one token at a time.
- [ ] Note the two phases of generation, which you'll optimize separately all project long:
  - **Prefill:** the whole prompt goes through the model at once.
  - **Decode:** one new token goes through the model per step.
- [ ] In your notes, write which phase you think is limited by compute and which by memory. You'll check your guess in week 7.
